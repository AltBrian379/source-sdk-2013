OWTF2 - Combat Medic (Ana-like support hero): plan
===================================================

Gameplay of Overwatch's Ana, recreated with an original name. Nothing from Blizzard
(names, models, sounds, art) goes into the mod.

Naming
------
- Display name: "Combat Medic".
- Code: TF_CLASS_COMBATMED (11, after Veteran; Civilian moves to 12, Random to 14).
  Raw name "combatmed", class script scripts/playerclasses/combatmed.txt.
- Functions CombatMed_*, classes CTFCombatMed*, convars tf_combatmed_* (FCVAR_REPLICATED |
  FCVAR_NOTIFY). Every tunable number is a convar.

Placeholders
------------
- Medic model and hitboxes.
- Base speed 280 (the Demoman's), like every ported hero.
- Max health 175, like the Veteran: Ana has Soldier: 76's 250 HP.
- Damage and healing: Ana's current values x 0.7 (175 / 250), the rule in CLAUDE.md
  ("Stat scaling for ported heroes"). TF2's modifiers (distance, crits, resistances) apply on
  top by default. Exception, set by the user: the Biotic Rifle is flat, no distance modifier on
  its damage or its healing (no close-range bonus, no falloff, no crossbow travel-time heal bonus).
  Ana's rifle has no falloff, and neither do TF2's sniper rifles.
- Ana's numbers (rifle damage and heal, fire rate, clip, cooldowns, durations, radii) must be
  checked against the live game before each phase. OW2 changed several of them.

Loadout and keys
----------------
  Slot 1  Biotic Rifle    +attack fires, +attack2 scopes.
  Slot 2  Biotic Grenade  key 2. Instant switch, throw animation, grenade leaves after a short
                          delay, then back to the rifle.
  Slot 3  Sleep Dart      +attack4 (SHIFT) or key 3. Switch, cast, fire, back to the rifle.
  -       Nano Boost      +attack5 (Q), the ultimate system.

Grenade and Dart block firing like a cast, so they're weapons the player switches to and
from (like the Veteran's Field Rations), not abilities fired from the rifle.
- Neither interrupts the other ability. Using the Dart right after the Grenade still returns
  to the rifle. Every cast returns to the rifle.
- Using either while scoped unscopes, and the scope does not come back.

Shared cast-weapon base
-----------------------
The Field Rations already do switch in -> cast -> effect -> cooldown -> switch back. Move that
into a base class (CTFCastAbilityWeapon) with settings for cast time, cooldown, instant deploy
and the animation to play. Then put the Field Rations, Grenade and Dart on it, so switch
blocking and the return to the rifle live in one place. The switch triggered by +attack4 must
be predicted (done from shared code, like the Field Rations' switch back).

Biotic Rifle
------------
- Built on CTFSniperRifle for zoom, scope overlay and FOV; no charge, no headshots.
- Unscoped: a projectile with the SYRINGE GUN's model (the crossbow bolt is too big and
  distracting). It's a new networked projectile. TF2's own syringes are partly client-side.
  It borrows the crossbow bolt's (CTFProjectile_HealingBolt) teammate-healing logic.
- Scoped: hitscan with a trace filter that includes teammates. Lag compensation must also
  rewind teammates for this weapon (CTFPlayer::WantsLagCompensationOnEntity; see whether the
  Medigun already has an exception there).
- Teammates: hits HURT teammates (health below max) and heals them. FULL-HEALTH teammates are
  ignored: the shot passes through them. This applies to both modes.
- Enemies: damage over a short time, with a small damage-over-time tracker on the victim
  (TF2's bleed has fixed ticks and doesn't fit).
- Healing credits ultimate charge through CTFGameStats::Event_PlayerHealedOther, after the
  heal modifier (blocked healing gives no charge).

Sleep Dart (slot 3)
-------------------
- Projectile with the CROSSBOW BOLT's model. Ignores teammates entirely (passes through).
- On an enemy hit: StunPlayer( TF_STUN_BOTH ) for the sleep duration, plus a sleep end time
  in CTFPlayerShared. Placeholder visuals: TF2's stun (stars, stun animation).
- Übercharge blocks it, like any bullet.
- Any damage wakes the target, damage over time included. TF2 applies the stun immediately,
  so there's no window between the hit and the sleep, and no exception is needed.
- It cancels an ultimate that's mid-cast, for ANY hero: a generic hook in tf_ultimate.cpp.
  The canceled ultimate's charge is lost.

Biotic Grenade (slot 2)
-----------------------
- Instant switch (no draw time), plays the throw animation; the grenade leaves after a short
  delay. Thrown like the Jarate (CTFProjectile_Jar, tf_weapon_jar.cpp). Explodes on impact.
- Allies in the area: heal, plus a heal-boost window.
- Enemies in the area: damage, plus a no-heal window.
- Tints: no-heal tints the player PURPLE, heal boost tints the player GREEN. Both are subtle,
  5-10%. Done like Jarate's yellow: the YellowLevel material proxy (CProxyUrineLevel,
  client/tf/c_tf_player.cpp), which player materials already use, gets a case for each state.
  Weapons and cosmetics whose materials use the proxy are tinted too.

Heal modifier (new logic; TF2 has no heal boost or full heal block)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
One // OWTF2: step that all incoming healing passes through, reading two networked end
times on the player (no-heal, heal boost) and a boost multiplier:
- CTFPlayer::TakeHealth: health packs, bolts, our projectiles.
- The healer-list tick in CTFPlayerShared (tf_player_shared.cpp, where TF2 already applies the
  afterburn heal reduction): Medigun, dispenser, the Veteran's healing field.
No-heal also blocks Nano Boost's burst heal. HUD icons for both states come later
(may need new assets).
Self-healing rules: no out-of-combat regeneration exists or is planned, so no special rule.

Nano Boost (ultimate)
---------------------
- Uses the existing ultimate system. CombatMed_CanUseUltimate requires a living ally under the
  crosshair (cone and line of sight, adapted from the Veteran's FindAutoAimTarget). The drain
  time is 0 (instant), and the effect runs on the ally for its duration.
- On the ally:
  - burst heal (blocked by no-heal);
  - TF_COND_ENERGY_BUFF (Crit-a-Cola's minicrits and its visual and audio cues);
  - TF_COND_DEFENSEBUFF_NO_CRIT_BLOCK (the Battalion's Backup resistance and its pulsing ring,
    without the crit immunity). Check it shows the ring.
- The Combat Medic is set as the conditions' provider, for assists.
- Resistance made modular: a per-application resistance value stored on the player
  (server-only, no DEFINE_PRED_FIELD), used by ApplyOnDamageModifyRules instead of the
  hard-coded 0.65. Stock sources leave it unset: 35%, and 50% against sentries, as before.
  Nano sets it from tf_combatmed_nano_resistance (default 0.35).
- Accepted differences from Overwatch: minicrits are +35% (not +50%) and remove the falloff
  penalty.

HUD
---
- Cooldown meters (Grenade, Dart) go in the row above the ultimate meter
  (CItemEffectMeterManager::PositionAboveUltimateMeter; done for the Veteran's HELIX/FIELD).
- Scope overlay (Sniper's), ultimate meter (existing), no-heal and heal-boost icons later.

Phases
------
0. DONE. Class skeleton: enum, tables (follow docs/veteran-class-changes.txt and the
   COMPILE_TIME_ASSERTs; grep TF_CLASS_ENGINEER, TF_CLASS_COUNT, TF_LAST_NORMAL_CLASS),
   LOADOUT_COUNT, combatmed.txt, class menu, three loadout slots, GetStockContentClassIndex.
1. Shared systems: the cast-weapon base (Field Rations moved onto it), teammate trace and lag
   compensation, damage-over-time tracker, heal modifier, modular defense-buff resistance,
   the generic "cancel ultimate cast" hook. DONE (see CLAUDE.md, "Shared hero systems").
2. Biotic Rifle. DONE (see CLAUDE.md).
3. Biotic Grenade (heal boost, no-heal, tints). DONE (see CLAUDE.md). Tints are about 20%
   (set by the user after seeing 5-10%).
4. Sleep Dart. DONE (see CLAUDE.md). Not ported: the shorter sleep on tanks (TF2 has none) and the
   0.5 s wake-up recovery.
5. Nano Boost. DONE (see CLAUDE.md). The Heavy's Buffalo Steak speed from the energy buff is
   skipped while Nano-boosted (set by the user).
6. Details: HUD icons, model, sounds, art, class-menu assets.

Testing: the VScript harness (CLAUDE.md). Healing tests need a teammate that stands still,
e.g. a puppet bot (`bot -team red -class heavyweapons`). Print health before and after each
hit to check heal, damage over time, no-heal, heal boost and Nano numbers.
